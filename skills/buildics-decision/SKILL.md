# BUILDICS Decision

1. Accept only version-compatible findings with source provenance and quality status. If evidence is insufficient, propose investigation rather than a control action.
2. Add context: equipment criticality, occupancy, maintenance history and safety constraints only when supplied or verified.
3. Produce at least two feasible options when possible, including monitor/inspect; score urgency and impact with explicit criteria, not opaque confidence numbers.
4. For each proposal include evidence IDs, assumptions, expected benefit, risk, reversible/irreversible status, responsible role and approval requirement.
5. Never command devices directly. Send proposals to Chief for authorization workflow; require explicit approval before Action.
6. Preserve rejected/uncertain decisions for Learning without converting them into automatic policy changes.
